# User walk of the app as a new seller · 2026-11-23

Task T-USER-WALK. The owner's request: use the app in Edge as a real user, from scratch; make a poster by Message
until its confirmation, the same by « Pas à pas », and one from a reference photo; note every friction (small ones
included), every possible simplification and every feature a seller would not use; weigh his five ideas.

## What was walked, and where

- **Build:** `main` at `ad6cacc` (the frontend build of 2026-11-23 00:04), not the released `05a12ee`.
- **Instance:** a local one started for this walk, http://127.0.0.1:5536, its own data folder `backend/data-walk`
  (git-ignored). Real providers on the owner's word: images `gpt-image-2` quality high, the reader of messages
  `claude-sonnet-5-5`. Facebook simulated (`stub`): no real Page, no real post.
- **Browser:** Edge 157 on the Linux laptop, desktop width (1874 px), then a 495 px window for the phone layouts (the
  narrowest this window goes). No real phone.
- **Shop:** a new one, `essaiclaude`, 15 offered credits, no profile, conditions not accepted. Sign-in details:
  `backend/data-walk/acces.txt`.
- **Spend:** 5 images, 1.06 USD (0.21 USD each), plus 0.05 USD of message reading. 5 credits of 15.
- **Posters made** (files in `outputs/user-walk/`):
  1. Sony WH-CH720N by Message, version 1 then version 2 after a correction, confirmed, « published » on the simulated Page.
  2. Marshall Willen II by « Pas à pas », style made from a reference poster, shop name and contact added, confirmed, « published ».
  3. Harman Kardon Aura Studio 4 by Message with a reference poster as « Inspiration », version 1 then version 2 from a
     free request. Left « à vérifier ».
- The first minutes (sign-in errors, conditions, « Plus tard », the guide, a message without a photo) were walked on the
  simulated trial instance (port 5526) before the owner asked for real providers.

**Not walked:** a real phone (Android or iPhone), the real Facebook dialog and a real post, the PNG download and the

un problème », refusing a version, an empty credit balance, a provider failure or refusal, a lost connection, the
operator's page. The table of refusal messages was not checked line by line.

**Checked mechanically:** the eight collapsed sections of the shop's screens (`<details>`) hold optional content only:
shop identity on the intake form, « Ajouter les informations manquantes », « Créer un style depuis une affiche »,

## The eleven findings that matter most

Ranked by what they cost a first-time seller.

### 1. A confirmed poster stays « À vérifier » (defect; the owner's remark on « Publier »)

The Sony poster was confirmed at version 2 and « published ». « Mes affiches », the menu's recent list and
« Accueil » still call it « À vérifier », after a reload too. « Accueil » keeps it under « Affiches à reprendre »
(« + 1 à vérifier »). On the same screen, « Publier » lists it under « Affiches confirmées ».

Cause (read in the code and in the data): `backend/src/affiche/api.py:560`, `TO_REVIEW`. Its second clause makes a
poster « to review » while **any** image of any of its jobs is still `pending_review`. Version 1 was never approved nor
refused: it was corrected by message. The confirmation of version 2 exists in `confirmations` for the current revision,
but `to_review` is tested before `confirmed` (`api.py:1076`). The ordinary Message path (version 1, a correction,
version 2, confirm) always ends in this state. The Marshall poster, confirmed at its only version, shows « Confirmée ».

Three words are in use for one idea: « Confirmée » (lists), « Approuvée » / « approuvée » (the editor's version
label), « à vérifier ».

### 2. Message drops what it has no field for, and does not say so

At creation the reader keeps a name, a price and a style. Everything else is dropped without a word:

- « ajoute garantie 6 mois et livraison gratuite à Tana. Je veux un fond rouge avec des flammes et le casque posé sur
  une table en bois » gave a second card identical to the first, except the style, changed from « Studio clair » to
  « Couleurs du produit » without saying it.
- « caractéristiques sur le côté : Bluetooth, 130 W, éclairage d'ambiance » left no trace on the card.
- « la deuxième photo n'est pas une inspiration, c'est aussi un produit » gave the same card again, under « Une
  erreur ? Écrivez la correction. »

The seller learns it only on the image, after one credit and one to two minutes. The image prompt of version 1 holds
none of these words (read in `poster_visuals.prompt_text`): the scene is the fixed house scene.

**But after the first image the same kind of sentence is accepted.** « Je veux un fond sombre avec des lumières de
fête, et les caractéristiques Bluetooth, 130 W écrites à droite avec des icônes » was taken whole as « Scène : … »,
cost one credit, and the result is the best Message poster of the walk: the dark scene, the lights, two icons with
« Bluetooth » and « 130 W ». So the free request works; it is only refused a place in the first message. Its warning
(« Vous demandez des caractéristiques sans les lister ? ») was shown although the two were listed.

### 3. A seller cannot write his own sale conditions on a poster

« et la garantie 6 mois ? je veux que ce soit écrit sur l'affiche » → « Je ne peux pas demander ce changement à
l'image : retirez les mots de prix, de promotion ou de garantie. Le prix se change à part, par message. »
The answer tells him to remove the word he wants. No screen offers another way: « Pas à pas » has a title, a brand and
a subtitle of 60 characters, and the title refuses « promo », « gratuit », « % » (`title_commercial_text`).

For the seller the rule looks arbitrary: « Bluetooth, réduction de bruit, 35h d'autonomie » (facts the app cannot
check either) went in as a subtitle without a question. And the pilot's own posts carry exactly these lines
(warranty, delivery, payment).

The pending title correction also lost its « Refaire l'affiche » button when that question was asked: it was cancelled
without a word.

### 4. The price is not read the way sellers write it

With the real reader: « casque sony wh-ch720n noir 250000 » → « Quel est le prix ? Écrivez-le en chiffres, par exemple
700 000 Ar. » (it was in digits). « 250k » → the same sentence again, with no hint of what was wrong. « 250 000 » →
accepted. « Pas à pas » accepted « 185000 ar » at once: two rules for one field.

### 5. The reference poster works very well in one place and not in the other

- « Pas à pas », Style tab, at the very bottom, collapsed: « Créer un style depuis une affiche ». The reference
  (a dark poster with orange waves) was described in French in about 8 seconds, free, saved as « Style 1 ». The
  generated poster follows it closely. It is the best result of the walk, and the most hidden control of the app.
- Message, a second photo marked « Inspiration »: the result is the grey house studio, nothing of the dark reference.
  The prompt does name the image as a style reference, but the house-scene sentence stays in it.

Same promise, two mechanisms, opposite results.

### 6. « Corriger l'affiche » throws the Message user into the editor

In the review opened from Message, « Corriger l'affiche » opens « Pas à pas »: tabs, colour codes, « Versions 1 sur
12 », a four-step bar, undo and redo, the menu reduced to icons. Someone who chose to write messages expects to write
the correction. Getting back needs the « Message » switch at the top.

### 7. A change of words costs a credit and redraws the product

« le titre c'est Casque Sony WH-CH720N avec les majuscules » → « Refaire l'affiche · 1 crédit », 90 seconds, and a new
scene: the headphones are drawn again, at another angle. The title is drawn by the image model by default (« Laisser
l'IA composer le titre dans l'image »), so no text change is free in Message. In the editor the shop name's colour
changed at once and free. The name was also kept as typed, in lower case (« casque sony wh-ch720n noir »): the first
correction a seller makes is one the app could have avoided.

### 8. « Publier »: the success is below the fold, the start is a dead end

- After « Publier », the page comes back to an empty composer with « Choisissez au moins une affiche. » in the warning
  colour. « Publié sur Page d'essai 1 · Voir la publication » is at the bottom of the page, out of view at 1874 × 941.
  It reads as a failure.
- With no Page connected, « Confirmer et préparer la publication » (the main button of the review) leads to « Aucune
  Page Facebook n'est connectée. Connectez votre Page dans « Mon compte ». » No « Affiche confirmée », no button to
  connect on that page. (The way back from « Mon compte », « Revenir à ma publication », works.)
- « Publier plusieurs affiches » drops the poster the seller came with; the picker is under the description, below the
  fold, while the page says « Choisissez vos affiches plus bas ».

### 9. The shop's name and contact: three steps, then an unreadable name

Type the contact in a collapsed block, « Ajouter à cette affiche » (which does not add it: « Activez uniquement
celles à afficher »), then switch it on. At once: « Deux éléments se superposent. Vérifiez leur lisibilité. », shown
three times during the generation. A click on it selects « Contact » and offers « Retirer de l'affiche »; it does not
say which two elements, and proposes no fix. On the finished poster the shop name was dark blue on a dark scene,
pressed against the price label: unreadable. The fix is in « Réglages » → « Couleur » (found only by looking for it).

### 10. Three photos are not three products

Three product photos sent together: the second is marked « Inspiration » by its position, and « Style : comme votre
inspiration » is chosen. Nothing asks. The sentence that says so afterwards is ignored (finding 2). The form says
« 1 à 3 photos » of one product; the image prompt asks for « one product set as a single large hero object ».

### 11. The wait

60 to 120 seconds per image. Message shows « Je dessine l'affiche… » and nothing else. « Pas à pas » shows three steps
and a clock, which is better. Neither says how long it usually takes.

## Simplifications

1. **One status word.** « Confirmée » everywhere; a poster whose current version is confirmed is never « À vérifier ».
2. **Say what was understood, and what was not.** Each card of Message lists what it kept and what it could not use
   (« Je n'ai pas utilisé : garantie 6 mois, fond rouge »), with the way to get it.
3. **Take the scene request in the first message.** The mechanism exists for the second image; use it for the first.
4. **One place for the seller's own lines** (warranty, delivery, payment), typed once for the shop, shown as he wrote
   them, never written by the model. It answers finding 3 without breaking « never invent ».
5. **One reference mechanism.** Message's « Inspiration » goes through the description that « Créer un style depuis
   une affiche » makes. Move that control to the top of the Style tab and to the « Styles » page.
6. **Corrections stay where the seller is.** In Message, « Corriger l'affiche » puts the cursor in the message field.
7. **Text changes without a new image** when the title is laid over the picture; when it is drawn, say before the
   first image that each word change costs one.
8. **Review: two buttons and one link.** « Télécharger » and « Publier sur ma Page » (shown only with a Page),
   « Corriger ». Confirmation is what either button does. The rest under « Autres actions », as « Affiche confirmée »
   already does.
9. **Publier: the result at the top**, the picker before the description, « Connecter ma Page » on the page itself.
10. **Prices: accept 250000, 250 000, 250.000, 250k, with or without « Ar »**, and answer « 250 000 Ar ? » with one
    button when unsure. One rule for Message and the form.
11. **Tidy the name** (capitals of brand and model) and replace a straight quote, instead of refusing.
12. **Accueil:** one credit figure, one start button, no « vérifié à » lines, no footer links.
13. **Shop identity once**, in « Mon compte »; on a poster, three switches. A light or dark text colour chosen from
    the scene.
14. **A duration** on the wait (« environ 1 minute »).
15. **A caption proposed** on « Publier » from the name, the price and the contact.

## What a seller would not use

- « Réglages »: Police, Taille, Graisse, Alignement, Contour, Ombre, Position X and Y, Largeur, Hauteur. One exception
  found useful: the text colour, which the app should choose itself.
- The colour codes (`#F5F5F5`), « Corriger la couleur principale », « Reprendre les couleurs de la photo », « Couleur
  d'accent » with « Autre… », « Mot du titre en couleur ».
- « Marque (facultative) » as a field apart from the title.
- The three « dessiner dans l'image » boxes: a seller cannot judge the choice; the app should make it.
- « Versions 0 sur 12 », « Choisie pour l'export », « Comparer », undo and redo, the four-step bar
  (« Préparation — Génération — Vérification — Fichier prêt »).
- « Variante confirmée » and the attestation box as separate inputs.
- « Position du contact », the chips « Scène / Titre / Prix » under the poster, « Déplacer les éléments ».
- « En attente 0 crédits », every « vérifié à HH:MM », « Actualiser ».
- « Confirmer et préparer le partage » beside « préparer la publication ».
- The « Styles » page as it is: a gallery that can do nothing.
- « Retirées » as a tab of « Mes affiches » (a rare need: a link is enough).

Not judged, because not walked: Carré and Story, the share sheet, « Dupliquer », « Signaler un problème ».

## The owner's five ideas, against what the walk showed

**1. Compositions with up to three images.** Today three photos mean one product seen three times, and in Message the
second is taken for a style reference (finding 10). A pack of three products is not possible: the prompt asks for one
hero object. The need is real (bundles, a phone with its accessories). It needs, at the least, a role chosen by the
seller for each photo (« même produit », « autre produit », « modèle de style ») before sending, and a scene sentence
that allows several objects. Not tried with the image model: no evidence yet that three products stay faithful.

**2. Free prompting in Message, the app as a bridge; or a guard.** The strongest evidence of the walk. The free
request gave the best Message poster, and it is already allowed, but only from the second image (finding 2). Opening
it in the first message removes a wasted credit per poster. The guard exists in part: commercial words are refused in
the scene. Recommendation: keep the three things the app must own (the product photo untouched, the price as an exact
number, the seller's own conditions as typed text) and let everything else of the message go to the scene. For
« only product or promotion images », one cheap question to the reader that already reads each message (is this an
advert for something the shop sells?) is enough for the pilot; nothing in the walk needed more.

**3. Clean « Pas à pas ».** Agreed; the list above is what to cut. What is left is small: photos, name, price, a
style (three provided, the shop's own, « depuis une affiche », « décrire »), the shop's lines, « Générer ». The
question it raises: with Message taking free requests, « Pas à pas » may be a form that fills the first message, not
a second editor.

**4. A visual step-by-step at first sign-in.** Today: a text dialog of four steps, not opened by itself, read before
any screen is known (S6). The first screen a new seller sees is the conditions. A guide that shows the real controls
one after the other, on the first poster, would answer it. Two findings to fix first, or the guide will have to
explain them: the price format and the dropped words.

**5. The status on « Publier » is still « À confirmer ».** Found and explained (finding 1): a defect of the server's
rule, not of the page. One clause to change and one test (version 1 pending, version 2 confirmed → « confirmed »).

## Direction asked by the owner after the report (2026-11-23): the platform as a kit

His words: « think the platform as a framework the user can use to speedup the creation: a title font he can use,
some scene model, etc., but they could still free prompt if that is what they want ». Before any fix. Concept
proposed by Claude the same day, **not approved, no requirements yet**; four decisions are his (end of this section).

**A poster = the product (photo, name, price) + four saved choices, each with a default, each replaceable by words.**

| Block | What it sets | Exists today | Missing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Scène | backdrop, light, how the product is staged | inside a « style » (`looks`: `direction_fr`, `surface_fr`, `lighting`, `camera`, `palette`): three provided, the shop's own from a poster, « Décrire moi-même » | chosen apart from the title; kept from one's own result |
| Titre | lettering, accent colour, alignment | inside the same style (`headline_style_en`, `accent_colour`, `alignment`); three real fonts in « Réglages » | named title styles, chosen apart |
| Mise en page | product alone, product with a list of features and icons, several products | fixed: one product, title above, price below | new; a paid trial first |
| Mentions | shop name, contact, logo, and the seller's own lines (warranty, delivery, payment) | name, contact, logo in « Mon compte » | the seller's lines; a text colour chosen from the scene |

Rules:
1. Each saved choice is readable words (as « Ambiance (modifiable) » today), never a locked template.
2. Order of what wins: this message, then this poster's choices, then the shop's kit, then the house defaults.
3. Three things stay the app's: the product as photographed, the price as an exact number, the seller's lines as typed.
4. Each card says what it will use from the kit and from the message, and what it could not use.

Screens: one way to create (the message box, with the kit's choices as chips); « Styles » becomes « Mon kit », where
choices are made and kept; « Pas à pas » becomes the correction of a finished poster. Findings 2, 3, 5, 6 and 10 of
this report are answered by this, not by separate fixes; findings 1, 4, 8 are independent.

Slices: (1) the seller's lines, the scene words in the first message, the card that says what it used, Message's
reference through the style description; (2) « Mon kit », scene and title chosen apart, « Garder comme modèle » from a
result, chips in the message box; (3) layouts, after a paid trial.

Decisions awaited: (a) the kit is the shop's only, or the operator also publishes models for all shops; (b) one way to
create, or Message and « Pas à pas » both; (c) the title drawn by the image model from a style (best look, a word
change costs a credit) or laid over in a real font (exact, free to change, plainer); (d) free words for the scene and
the layout only, the three things of rule 3 kept by the app.
