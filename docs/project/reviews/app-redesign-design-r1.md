# App redesign — design review, round 1 (Codex, 2026-11-20)

Reviewed: `designs/app-redesign.md` v1. Verdict: REJECT. Answered by v2.

**REJECT v1.** The approved visual direction is implementable. The blockers are in the shell and data contracts. As written, the redesign can move the phone’s approval buttons off the viewport, return focus to the wrong credits control, promise shop information that a cold Accueil cannot read, and show an operator that nothing needs attention when a read failed. I would revise the design before starting the implementation waves.

Line references to **v1** below are to [app-redesign.md](../../../docs/project/designs/app-redesign.md).

But v1 lines 107–116 and 203 also prescribe blur on the review and final panels. On phones, `.review-panel` is itself the scrolling ancestor of `.review-panel .actions`, whose approval buttons are fixed to the viewport. Putting `backdrop-filter` on that panel changes their containing block. Today the panel uses an ambient background and a large scrim shadow. Its action bar has the blur. Preserve that arrangement or paint the panel effect on a non-containing pseudo-element.
