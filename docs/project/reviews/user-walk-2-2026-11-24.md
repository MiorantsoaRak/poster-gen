## Fixed during the walk

**The first image ignored the scene words.** Poster 1 came out as the grey house studio, although the recap showed
« avec vos mots » and the prompt held them as `kept_adjustments`. Cause: the scene sentence still led with the house
style (« House style, unless … the kept adjustments say otherwise »), beside a pale palette; a refinement follows such
words because its base image carries the scene. Fix (`backend/src/affiche/posters/plans.py`, `compile_scene`): on an
image made from nothing, with owner's words, the scene sentence leads with them (« the owner described the picture he
wants … Follow them first, even where they differ from the palette »), and the constraint against props excepts what
he asked for. Tests updated (`test_plans.py`, `test_poster_visuals.py`). Poster 3, same words, after the fix: red
backdrop, flames, the headphones on a wooden table.

## After the walk: the owner's own trial (2026-11-24)

On poster 4 the owner wrote « Aura studio on the center ». The chat answered « Scène : « Aura studio on the center » »
and drew a version 2 with the three products where they were, a studio glow in the centre and the wooden table gone:
one credit for nothing. Causes and fix: T-KIT-020 (the request was not said as an instruction and lost against
« preserve its composition »; nothing said that a name can be one of the products). After the fix, the same words on
the same poster: the Aura Studio in the centre, larger, the table back, the title, lines and price as before
(`outputs/user-walk-2/05-pack-aura-centre-after-fix.png`). One image, 0.21 USD.

The app still does not know the name of each product of a pack: only the image model recognises « Aura Studio ».
The owner decided the same day that this is the rule (the image model decides, no prompt is blocked). What it does
not fix: nothing tells the seller when a paid correction changed nothing.

## Verdict

For a seller who types as in the first walk, the app now does in one message and one credit what took three credits
and still missed the warranty. The reference photo and the scene words give the two best posters of both walks from
Message alone. What still needs the owner's eye before a release: the size of the drawn lines on a phone (W4), the
double post (W7), and a first real seller on a real phone (W1).
