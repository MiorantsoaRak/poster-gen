# First-poster tutorial

**Status** Approved for design, 2026-11-22, on the owner’s « continue » after the requirements handoff. [French walkthrough for the current release](../guide-premiere-affiche.md).

| ID | Acceptance criterion |
|---|---|
| R-GUIDE-001 | After the first terms choice, a new shop can open « Ma première affiche » or go straight to work. Returning users can reopen it from Help. Closing or reopening it preserves the active draft, chat attachments, chosen images and description. |
| R-GUIDE-002 | In French, guide the shop through the original photo and verified offer, the format/style and credit decision, one comparison of the exact rendered poster with the product photo and offer, and a choice of PNG, direct Page post or device sharing. Include the alternative Pas à pas route and ordered multi-image publishing. Control names match the release in both layouts. |
| R-GUIDE-003 | Reading, advancing or replaying the guide never starts a model call, accepts terms, changes a poster, connects a Page or sends a post. A person who chooses to work on a real poster uses the normal explicit actions for each consequence. No forced tour or overlay blocks the app. |
| R-GUIDE-004 | At 360 px with keyboard open and on desktop, each instruction is reachable by keyboard and assistive technology in a logical order. Help for a missing confirmed file, expired connection or uncertain send points to the applicable recovery action. A seller can complete a guided stub PNG and stub Facebook scenario without facilitator instructions. |
