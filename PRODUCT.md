# Doka Studio

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary user is the owner or staff member of an invited shop in Madagascar. They have a product to sell, a real photo and a verified price. They need a French promotional poster they can check, export and reuse. Original Phone Mada is the pilot shop. A service operator manages invitations and prepaid credits.

## Product Purpose

Help the shop turn a verified offer into a poster it is willing to publish. Judge success by the time and assistance needed to reach an accepted poster, the corrections the owner has to make, and whether the shop returns for its next offer. The pilot has no agreed numerical target for those measures.

## Positioning

The shop can describe an affiche in French or work through the steps. Both paths lead to the same creation. The owner can compare a generated scene with the original photo, correct the composition and review the final file. A saved poster gives the next offer a starting point.

## Operating Context

An owner can start and finish on a phone or desktop. A typical job begins with a photo, product details and a price in ariary. The owner chooses a direction, generates a version, compares the exact rendered poster with the original product and price, then downloads it or prepares a Page post. Shops publish on Facebook, as the shop's Page, and on WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business. They work mostly on Android phones in Chrome, often on mobile data. Accueil surfaces posters needing attention. Crédits explains available and pending credits and their history. Mon compte holds the shop identity and access settings.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve original photos and the shop’s verified offer. Prices, discounts, warranties and specifications require the owner’s confirmation. AI-generated details need review before publication.
- Text and effects can be editable layers. A product integrated into a generated scene stays part of its image. When the price is drawn into that image, a price change requires a new scene and review.
- Release 1 serves invited shops with prepaid generation credits managed by the operator. Public signup, subscription tiers and automated payments belong to later scope.
- Release 1 is live since 2026-11-20 with the pilot shop only. New shops are invited once « Partager » has worked on a real phone.
- « Confirmer pour plus tard » keeps the reviewed PNG in Mes affiches. « Confirmer et télécharger le PNG » confirms and downloads it in one action. Later downloads use « Télécharger le PNG ». When Page publishing is enabled, « Confirmer et préparer la publication » accepts the reviewed Publication-format image and opens its composer for the connected Page. « Publier » supports several confirmed images in the owner's chosen order. The owner reviews the Page, description and images before sending.
- On a compatible phone, « Partager dans une autre application » opens the sharing controls. « Partager » hands a JPEG to the phone's share sheet and attempts to copy the caption. The owner finishes in the chosen app. The PNG remains available if sharing fails.
- A shop accepts the conditions of use before any new AI request. Editing, checking and downloading work without that acceptance.
- The current interface and captions are French. Malagasy localization is a later decision.

## Product Principles

1. Put the original photo and confirmed offer within reach when the owner judges a generated version.
2. Explain the credit cost before the owner commits to an image. Distinguish a request awaiting a result from a completed spend.
3. Preserve work across interruptions. A shop owner should be able to check their balance and return to the same draft and attachments.
4. Give the shop the final say on what it publishes. Make the next required check clear and keep earlier versions available for comparison.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support the full workflow on small phones and desktop, in both themes. At 360 px wide, keep actions reachable with the keyboard open. Provide keyboard navigation, visible focus and meaningful screen-reader labels. Distinguish a real zero from a figure that failed to load. Physical-phone and screen-reader verification remain release checks.
